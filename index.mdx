## Add capabilities as your agent needs them

An agent researching a company might need web search, page extraction, or business data. AgentMuxer lets it discover those capabilities during the task, inspect the available tools, and make a purchase within your spending controls.

You keep your existing model and agent framework. AgentMuxer provides access to tools; it does not supply the model.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Sign in from your personal agent, or create an application key for an agent you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a tool">
    Your agent searches the marketplace and checks a tool's inputs and price. Searching does not spend credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result">
    Your agent calls the tool and uses its result in your task. Review purchases in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
