## Add capabilities as your agent needs them

Your agent finds the tools it needs as it works, checks their inputs and prices, and uses them within your spending controls.

Keep using your preferred model and agent framework.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Sign in from your personal agent, or create an application key for an agent you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a tool">
    Your agent searches the marketplace and checks a tool's inputs and price. Searching does not spend credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result">
    Your agent calls the tool and uses its result in your task. Review purchases in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
